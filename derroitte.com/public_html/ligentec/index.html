Monitoring the electrical grid, including decentralised energy sources, gives a much more accurate picture of the network, which in turn supports better electricity production and a greater use of renewable energy.

02
Iterate to rebuild trust

I took over the project in a difficult political context: a first “data product”, after a previous team that had not delivered. I chose a very iterative approach: ship a first piece quickly to reassure stakeholders. Today I lead 2 data products and contribute to the architecture of 2 more.

→ in parallel since early 2026: Faction Lead DataOps, a team of 15.

Why LIGENTEC

Closer to the product

I studied computer science engineering at ULiège, focusing on intelligent systems and robotics. I want to get closer to the kind of work that drew me to engineering in the first place. At LIGENTEC, I could build a data platform that helps engineers make decisions about how photonic chips are developed and manufactured. That’s the kind of impact I want my work to have.

Build it and see it through

At Elia, I saw how rewarding it is to build data products that the control room can use in its daily work. I want to stay hands-on, take responsibility for the platform’s technical direction, and work with the same engineering teams long enough to see what holds up in practice.

Ready for a new adventure

Changing priorities at Orange Business have made the direction of my role less predictable lately. I’ve also been thinking seriously about moving to Lausanne, where I already have friends and would like to build a life. The chance to work closer to an engineering product and make that move is what made me knock on LIGENTEC’s door.
